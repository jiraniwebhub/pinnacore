🚚 Delivery Information

We provide fast and reliable delivery services within Nairobi and selected regions across Kenya. Our goal is to ensure your order reaches you safely and on time.

Delivery Options

  • Nairobi Same-Day Delivery: Orders placed before 2:00 PM
  • Next-Day Delivery: Outside Nairobi areas
  • Pickup: Available upon request

Delivery fees depend on your location and will be confirmed before dispatch via WhatsApp.

💳 Payment Methods

  • M-Pesa (Preferred method)
  • Cash on Delivery (Nairobi only)
  • Bank Transfer (on request)

Payments are confirmed before order dispatch for security and transparency.

📦 Returns & Refunds

We accept returns within 7 days of purchase under the following conditions:

  • Item must be unused and in original packaging
  • Proof of purchase is required
  • Refunds are processed after inspection

Damaged or used items are not eligible for return.
